Composition/framing: centered square app icon, mark occupies around 78 percent of canvas, no surrounding badge or app-store frame.
Text: none, symbol only.
Constraints: original design; no Minecraft grass block, creeper face, Mojang mark, NVIDIA mark, existing MultiCraft/MultiMC logo, gradients that obscure shape, drop shadow, watermark, letters other than the implicit M, decorative sparkles, tiny details.
